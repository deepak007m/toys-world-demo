Store Pickup & Fulfilment

Toys World & Gift Gallery operates a physical toy and gift showroom located on Manor Highway Road, Palghar, Maharashtra. Customers are welcome to visit our showroom in person or enquire about product availability through WhatsApp.

Enquiring via WhatsApp

When you click “Order on WhatsApp” or “Enquire on WhatsApp” on any catalogue item, you connect directly with our store team. We confirm whether the product is currently in stock, verify pricing, and discuss available pickup or handover arrangements.

In-Store Collection & Delivery Inquiries

Customers can confirm items with us via WhatsApp and arrange to collect them directly at our Palghar store. If you require delivery or courier arrangements, please confirm availability, feasibility, and applicable terms directly with our team before purchasing, as options depend on the item and store schedule.
