Inspection & Quality Assurance

At Toys World & Gift Gallery, we take pride in offering genuine toys, action figures, and collectibles. We strongly encourage customers to inspect products in person at our Palghar showroom prior to purchase.

Exchanges & Support Inquiries

If you experience any issue or manufacturing defect with a product purchased from Toys World, please reach out to our team promptly via WhatsApp or visit our store with your purchase receipt. We review items individually and assist with an appropriate exchange or resolution in accordance with store terms.

Collectible & Sealed Items

For collector items such as sealed Hot Wheels cards, die-cast models, and mint anime figures, please verify packaging and condition upon pickup. Please confirm return or exchange eligibility with our team before purchasing collectible items.
