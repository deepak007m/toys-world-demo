FAQ

Last updated: Oct 2026

Where is Toys World located?

Our store is located on Manor Highway Road, opposite Holy Spirit High School, near the Royal Enfield Showroom, Tembhode, Mahim, Palghar, Maharashtra 401404.

How do I enquire about or purchase a product?

You can browse our digital catalogue online and tap the "Order on WhatsApp" or "Enquire on WhatsApp" button on any product. This connects you directly with the shop owner to confirm availability, pricing, and arrangements.

Can I visit the store in person?

Yes, absolutely! We welcome children, families, and collectors to visit our physical showroom in Palghar to explore anime figures, Hot Wheels die-cast cars, remote control vehicles, soft toys, and gift items in person.

What should I do if a product shows "Price on Request"?

Certain special collectibles and newly arrived pieces have fluctuating availability. Simply click the WhatsApp link on the product page and our team will share the exact price and product details.
